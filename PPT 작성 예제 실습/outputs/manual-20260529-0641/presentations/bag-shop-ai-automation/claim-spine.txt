thesis:
Women's handbag ecommerce teams can reduce operational drag by turning product detail checks, customer inquiries, reviews/returns, and weekly reports into a repeatable AI-assisted workflow.

audience:
SMB owner/operator of a women's handbag online shop without an internal development team.

one-line arc:
The deck moves from ecommerce friction and handbag-specific workload to Codex/n8n workflows, then closes with a 4-week adoption path and next decision.
